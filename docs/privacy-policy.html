Introduction

Velum ("we," "us," "our") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of data we collect, how it is utilized, and the measures we employ to secure it when you access our Platform.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our data management practices, please terminate your account immediately.

Security Model: Our storage utilizes AES-256-GCM symmetric encryption for high-risk attributes at rest, and credential verification utilizes Argon2id password-hashing with unique salts. No plaintext passwords or decryption keys are ever sent to or processed by the server database.

1. Information Collection

1.1 User-Provided Information

In operating your account, we handle:

  • Cryptographic Digests: Zero-knowledge hashes of your password, Safe Word, lockdown phrase, and recovery key. We do not store plaintext credentials.
  • Profile Attributes: Optional avatar selections, display name, public biography, and derived location.
  • Communications: Message contents, files, and ticket support conversations. Private communications are encrypted and isolated within active sessions.
  • Transactions: Escrow transaction records, marketplace item descriptions, and user feedback ratings.
  • Identity Verification (KYC): Where required to enable withdrawals or higher transaction limits, we collect government-issued identification, verification selfies, and other identity data through our verification process. This data is used solely to satisfy identity-verification and anti-money-laundering obligations.
  • Payment & Financial Data: Tokenized payment method references (we do not store raw card numbers), linked external account identifiers, wallet balances, and transaction history. Payment credentials are tokenized by our payment processor; the Platform does not retain raw payment instrument data.

1.2 Automatically Collected System Metadata

  • Hardware Information: Browser type, screen geometry, client user agent, and device fingerprints used for fraud prevention.
  • IP Address Logs: Captured to enforce sliding-window rate limits, detect session hijacking, and block unauthorized access.
  • Telemetry: Session duration, login attempts, activity timestamps, and message volumes.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use only strictly necessary session and authentication cookies/local storage required for login state and security. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If analytics or additional tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Policy will be updated in advance and you will be notified per Section 8.

4. Security Architecture & Retention

3.1 Encryption & Protection

Data at rest is secured via AES-256-GCM. Sessions are authenticated using cryptographic challenges containing one-time nonces to prevent credential replay attacks. Brute-force attacks are thwarted using exponential rate-limiting backoffs.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell user data. Information is disclosed only under the following strictly defined scenarios:

  • Legal Enforcement: Where legally mandated by Taiwanese regulatory authorities or courts.
  • Escrow Resolution: To Platform mediators in the event of a transaction dispute.
  • Identity Verification Providers: KYC documents are shared only with our identity-verification processor, solely to complete verification.
  • Payment Processors: Tokenized payment data is shared with our payment processor to execute transactions; the Platform never receives raw card or bank credentials.
  • Technical Service Providers: Encrypted payloads are stored with our database hosting provider (Neon/PostgreSQL) and object storage provider (Cloudflare R2) under strict data processing agreements.

International Transfers: Our infrastructure providers may process or store data outside your country of residence. Where this involves transfers out of the EU/EEA or other regions with data transfer restrictions, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards with those providers.

6. Your Rights

Users have the right to request access, correction, or permanent erasure of their account data. To request data deletion or export, please submit a support request or contact our data protection representative at the email below.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights:

  • EU/EEA/UK residents (GDPR): Right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): Right to know what personal information is collected, right to delete, right to correct, and right to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

7. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will terminate the account and delete associated data.

8. Changes to This Policy & Breach Notification

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in advance via account notification or email. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities without undue delay, consistent with applicable law.
