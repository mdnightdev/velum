Velum Corporation

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

1. Overview & Data Controller Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Velum Corporation ("Velum", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, protects, and retains personal data through the Velum platform and services. Velum Corporation operates as the primary data controller under applicable privacy laws, including the laws of Taiwan (R.O.C.) and international data protection standards (such as GDPR and CCPA).

2. Categories of Information Collected

We process only data necessary to operate, protect, and deliver Platform functionality:

  • Account & Authentication Credentials: Usernames, salted cryptographic hashes of passwords, Safe Words, Emergency Panic Phrases, and Recovery Keys. Plaintext secret keys or passwords are never stored.
  • Communications & Content: User messages, channel activity, attached media, support submissions, and marketplace listings.
  • Operational Metadata: Device identifiers, IP addresses, session tokens, and system event logs recorded during platform interactions.

3. Legal Bases & Processing Purposes

Data processing is conducted strictly under recognized legal bases:

  • Contract Performance: To deliver communication channels, escrow transactions, and account management.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain security, enforce terms, prevent fraudulent activity, and ensure service availability.
  • Legal Compliance: To satisfy mandatory legal obligations under governing law.

4. Data Security Controls

Velum Corporation implements strict technical safeguards to secure personal data:

  • In-Transit Encryption: All client-server communications are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS / WSS).
  • At-Rest Encryption: Database records, message contents, and stored files are encrypted at rest using server-managed symmetric encryption (AES-256-GCM).
  • Credential Hashing: Passwords and security phrases are salted and hashed server-side using memory-hard key derivation algorithms (Argon2id / SHA-256).

5. Data Retention & Erasure Timelines

Personal data is retained only for the duration required to satisfy legal, security, and operational requirements:

  • Standard Account Deletion Schedule: When a user initiates account deletion, all personal data, communications, and profile parameters enter a purging pipeline and are permanently erased within 14 days.
  • Expedited Asset Release Purge: Upon explicit user request to facilitate asset transfer and release, data erasure is accelerated and executed within 7 days or less.
  • Fraudulent Account Security Wipe: Accounts terminated or flagged for fraudulent activity, security exploitation, or system abuse undergo an immediate security purge and data wipe to prevent ongoing network risk.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Vendors

Velum Corporation does not sell, rent, or monetize personal data. Third-party data sharing is restricted to:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Host servers, databases, and object storage vendors operating under confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Legal Enforcement: Disclosure required pursuant to a valid, binding order issued by a court of competent jurisdiction.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have rights to request access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, and data portability. Requests can be submitted directly via internal support tickets or contact email.

8. International Operations & Jurisdiction

Velum Corporation's engineering and operations are managed under the jurisdiction of Taiwan (Republic of China / R.O.C.). Data processed by the Platform is safeguarded according to Taiwanese data protection regulations and applicable international standards.
